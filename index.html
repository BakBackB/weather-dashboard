Synchronized Precipitation–Humidity Trend

Color identifies city. Solid lines show precipitation (mm); dashed lines show humidity (%). The precipitation and humidity values for each month in the original chart represent the averages for that specific month over the period from 2015 to 2025. For monthly data, the values are averages of each day's measurements. (Example: the value of precipitation and humidity of June, 2020 is the average of all dates in that month)
