Humidity and Daily Temperature Range

Small dots: single days · Large dots: monthly averages (letter = month) · Dashed line: trend · R²: share of the daily-range differences that humidity explains. Hover a day for its values, click it for all cities, drag a box to zoom (double-click resets).

Humidity predicts the daily range well only in Ho Chi Minh City (R² ≈ 0.7): there, dry also means cloud-free (dry season) and humid means cloudy (rainy season). In Hanoi, winter days with the same humidity can be grey drizzle (small range) or sunny (large range), and Da Nang by the sea is humid almost all year, so humidity explains little there. Clouds are the common driver.
